Penfold Terms

Penfold’s role is to arrange your pension for you. This means everything from explaining what you need to know about pensions, helping you open one, helping you decide how much to save, and providing a range of features that help you manage your pension in the best way possible over your lifetime. It also means bringing together all the partners that make your pension work behind the scenes.

Please read and accept our terms and conditions document below which outlines in more detail our service and how this interacts with our partners. Please remember though Penfold should always be your first (and only) port of call for anything that you would like to do with your pension.

Scheme Administrator Terms

Your pension sits inside a scheme that is run day to day by a scheme administrator. They keep the scheme’s records, claim tax relief on your payments and make sure the scheme is run within the rules. These terms set out what they are responsible for and what they need from you.

Scheme Administrator Terms and Conditions

Key Features Document

The Key Features Document is a summary of the pension you are about to open. It covers what the pension is for, what you commit to when you pay in, and the risks that come with investing over a long time.

It also explains how payments and tax relief work, when you can take your money out, what happens to your pension if you die, and how to change your mind after you have opened it.
