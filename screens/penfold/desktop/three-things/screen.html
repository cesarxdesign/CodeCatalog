Could you live on £8,767 a year 😐? That’s currently the maximum amount the government gives you in the state pension after reaching retirement age, which for you would be 68. The amount could be less if you haven’t been paying in national insurance all your life.

Most people think that’s not enough so need another source of income after they stop work. The most common and tax efficient way to do this is usually though a pension.

After you turn 55, you can convert whatever you have put aside in a pension during your working years into an annual income for the rest of your life. It could make all the difference in how you spend over a third of your lifetime!

The government thinks pensions are great too, so most of the time when you pay in, they top it up with more free money based on how much tax you pay. We’ve worked out how much you could get based on what you earn.

You won’t get these government top-ups if you pay in more than everything you earned in a year, or more than £40,000. Tax benefits like this do depend on your individual circumstances and might change in the future. If you are a higher rate tax payer your basic rate tax is added by the pension provider, but you have to claim the higher rate tax back on your tax return and then add this to your pension.
