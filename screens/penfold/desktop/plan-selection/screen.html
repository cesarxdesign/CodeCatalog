Pick your risk level

Each of our plans has options for how much ‘risk’ you want to take. Basically, higher risk means your pot potentially making more Einstein Money over the long term, but with more ups and downs along the way. Low risk might mean nice and steady growth with fewer bumps but possibly less money overall.

We let you stay in total control of your money by picking which risk level works best for you. Usually, people with several decades until retirement pick a higher risk rating, then switch to a lower rating when they get closer to stopping work. See our FAQ on risk for more information.

Pick which one you think would be best for you based on the below guidance. You can switch anytime.

Risk Acceptance

Before we continue we need you to understand 3 things:

1.You may receive less money than you expect

In short, you could get back less than you invested. Neither we nor the money manager nor anyone else can remove this risk completely. The money manager’s goal is to reduce the chance of this happening and appropriately compensate you for the level of risk you are taking by keeping your costs low, diversifying your investments so you aren’t affected by a specific company or market event and allocating you to the right mix of types of investment.

2.Exchange rates can affect how much your money grows

There is a possibility that currency fluctuations will negatively affect the value of your investments, although the money manager works hard to reduce the impact of this.

3.Early withdrawals could impact your returns

Early and unforeseen withdrawals could reduce the amount your money grows. Generally the longer you hold an investment, the more opportunity there is for it to grow in value. Remaining focused on the long-term avoids being penalised by negative short and medium term market moves. You should have a long time horizon. It is generally a good idea to choose a lower risk level as you get closer to the time you can withdraw your money, to reduce the chance of short term drops in value affecting the size of your pot.

*Your investments are covered by the government’s Financial Services Compensation Scheme up to a limit of £85,000. Please be aware that this does not cover a fall in the value of your investment.
